# Text to paste on Etsy

Etsy strips out live links and buttons from most fields, so paste your tool's
web address as plain text (e.g. `https://yourname.github.io/swallowbirdcircle-design-tool/`)
— Etsy will usually turn it into a clickable link automatically once the
listing is live.

Replace `[YOUR LINK HERE]` below with your real GitHub Pages link once it's up.

---

## For your listing description

Add this near the top of any listing where buyers choose their own colors:

> **Design your own — no guesswork needed!**
> Lights out and away we go! Head to my design tool at [YOUR LINK HERE] and
> pick your bead colors right off my actual bead tray, then fill in the race
> car template bead-by-bead until it's exactly what you want. When you're
> done, download your design and add it as your personalization photo (or
> send it to me in a message) so I know exactly what to make.

## For the "Personalization" instructions field

> Want to pick your own colors? Visit [YOUR LINK HERE], build your design,
> and upload the downloaded picture here. Otherwise, just let me know your
> team or color preference and I'll make my pick.

## For your Shop Announcement / shop homepage

> Hi F1 fans! Ever since 2019 I've been hooked on racing and motorsports,
> and now you can design your own piece the easy way. Visit
> [YOUR LINK HERE], fill in your race car with colors from my actual bead
> stash, and send me the picture when you order. Simply lovely, made just
> for you.

## For a message reply template (when a buyer asks for a custom color)

> Thanks for reaching out! The easiest way to show me exactly what you'd
> like is my design tool: [YOUR LINK HERE]. Pick your colors, fill in the
> template, and download the picture — then attach it here or add it to your
> order notes. I probably have opinions on team colors too, so feel free to
> ask. :)

### A couple of Etsy specifics worth knowing

- Etsy does **not** allow embedded tools, scripts, or apps directly inside a
  listing or in chat — this is a platform-wide restriction, not something
  you can turn on. The link-out approach above is the standard workaround.
- Etsy's **personalization field** at checkout is the cleanest place to catch
  a buyer's uploaded design photo, if your listing has one enabled.
- If a buyer can't or doesn't want to use the tool, keep a normal "tell me
  your color preference in the notes" option available too — not everyone
  will want to use a web tool, and that's okay.
